## Who can apply

Students from Jamaat member families who have passed the 10th standard (SSC, CBSE, ICSE or equivalent) and are seeking admission to 11th Science (FYJC) in a junior college.

## Details needed for the application

1. Student's full name and mother's name as on the 10th marksheet.
2. Board, seat number and month and year of passing the 10th examination.
3. Marks in each subject, total marks and percentage.
4. Name of the school where the 10th standard was completed.
5. Preferred junior colleges and stream (Science).

## Documents required

1. 10th standard marksheet.
2. School leaving certificate.
3. Jamaat membership card of the student or the head of the family.
4. Two passport-size photographs.
5. Income proof of the family, if applying for fee assistance.

## How to apply

1. Upload the 10th marksheet in My Documents in Wasila. The marksheet details are then filled in automatically.
2. Ask the Wasila helpdesk to start the 11th Science admission application, and add your preferred colleges.
3. The Education Committee reviews the application and helps with college admission and fee assistance.
